Global Import–Export Operations

Trusted Trade Across Key International Markets

At FlairList Global, we operate as a cross-border trade, import–export, and market-expansion partner for businesses looking to trade reliably, compliantly, and profitably across multiple international markets.

Our strength lies not just in moving goods, but in understanding markets, buyers, regulations, and execution on ground.

We work with manufacturers, traders, MSMEs, bulk suppliers, and institutional buyers to enable smooth domestic and international trade flows—with clarity, structure, and accountability.

Countries We Actively Operate In

We currently support import, export, buyer sourcing, trade execution, and market expansion across the following key geographies:

IN India AE UAE VN Vietnam GB United Kingdom NL Netherlands US United States RU Russia KE Kenya

India

Base of Operations

India is our core operational base and supply origin for multiple global markets.

India acts as the manufacturing and sourcing backbone for our global trade network.
