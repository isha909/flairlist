Consultation 1

Business Understanding & Export Readiness Call

Market Value: ₹35,000

To diagnose export readiness and identify exact blockers before any strategy is suggested. This call ensures you don't enter exports blindly.

1. Business & Product Understanding

We map:
  • Product category & variant mapping
  • HS Code identification & validation
  • Product usage, shelf life & packaging suitability
  • Production capacity & scalability check
  • MOQ feasibility for international buyers

2. Commercial Readiness

We analyse:
  • Domestic pricing & margin analysis
  • Export pricing feasibility (FOB / CIF / CNF)
  • Cost breakup — manufacturing, logistics, duties, packaging
  • Currency fluctuation & buffer assessment

Understanding real cost vs perceived export price is the first unlock.

3. Export Readiness Assessment

We review:
  • IEC availability check
  • Certification review (FSSAI, ISO, CE, etc.)
  • Compliance & documentation gaps
  • Packaging & labeling compliance review
  • Country-wise regulatory sensitivity

Most exporters skip this — and face rejection at the border.

4. Market & Geography Understanding

We evaluate:
  • Countries you're considering
  • Countries you must avoid initially
  • Demand vs saturation analysis
  • Risk profiling — payment, compliance, logistics

Targeting the wrong country is the most expensive mistake in exports.

5. Blocker Identification

We uncover:
  • Why exports didn't start earlier
  • Why past inquiries didn't convert
  • Where time or money was wasted
  • Operational vs strategic blockers

Before strategy, we need to know what's actually in the way.
