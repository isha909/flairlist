CONSULTATION 1

Business Diagnosis Call

Market Value: ₹30,000

This is not a casual discussion. This is a business X-ray.

1. Business Model Diagnosis

We break down:
  • What you sell
  • Who you sell to
  • How money actually flows
  • Where margins are made or lost
  • Which part of the business is overworked vs underperforming

Most clients realize: “We were focusing on the wrong part of the business.”

2. Customer & Demand Reality Check

We analyse:
  • Who actually buys from you
  • Who should buy from you
  • Where demand is real vs assumed
  • Why current leads are low quality

This alone helps stop wasted marketing spend.

3. Sales & Conversion Bottleneck Mapping

We identify:
  • Where leads are dropping
  • Why inquiries don't convert
  • Where sales conversations fail
  • Why discounts are becoming necessary

Often, revenue improves without increasing leads.

4. Pricing & Value Leak Identification

We evaluate:
  • Whether pricing matches value
  • If margins are sustainable
  • If customers are confused, not price-sensitive
  • Whether objections are real or positioning-related

Most businesses don't need lower prices. They need clear value communication.

OUTCOME OF CONSULTATION 1 You leave with one powerful realization: “Now I know exactly what is broken—and why growth feels stuck.”
